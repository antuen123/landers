Do I need insurance?

No. The program is cash pay, so there are no insurance approvals, copays or surprise bills. What you see at checkout is what you pay.

How do I qualify for the medication?

Simply fill out the questionnaire and one of our USA-licensed doctors will review your answers and prescribe the medication if you qualify.

Do I pay anything before I know I am approved?

No. You will not be charged anything until a doctor approves your prescription. Payments are placed on hold at checkout and only captured once your prescription is sent to the pharmacy after doctor approval.

What if I want to talk to a doctor after signing up?

Our patient portal lets you message your doctor 24/7, and our support team is available 7 days a week.

Is blood work required?

Blood work is not required, but it is always recommended. You may submit recent blood work with your medical intake form if you have it.

What if I am on weight loss medications already?

We welcome patients who are already taking weight loss medications. The transition to our program is simple and seamless, just leave it to us.

How does the subscription work?

Your medication is shipped automatically every month, with no need to reorder. You are notified before each shipment, and you can cancel at any time. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees.
